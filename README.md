# The Spare Room 🛋️🌱
> **A little extra space for your brain.**

*The Spare Room* is a gentle external space for your working memory.

The idea is simple:
> **You don't have to hold everything in your head.**

Designed for people who struggle with working memory, mental clutter, executive dysfunction, getting started, or simply having too many thoughts at once. It does not feel like a traditional productivity app—it feels like a quiet, cozy room where you can put things down.

---

## ✨ Design Philosophy

* **You don't have to remember everything.**
* **You don't have to do everything today.**
* **Starting is enough.**
* **"Not today" is not a failure.** (Postponing a task quietly parks it without guilt or red overdue alerts).
* **Your feelings don't have to become tasks.**
* **Your brain deserves somewhere to put things down.**
* **Zero Guilt Guarantee:** No streaks, no points, no badges, and no noisy confetti celebrations.

---

## 🧭 Main Areas

### 🏠 1. Home
* **Time-aware greeting** and grounding thought.
* **Mood check-in** (😵💫 Overwhelmed, 🫠 Stuck, 😴 Low energy, 😐 Okay, ✨ Good) with restorative affirmations and adaptive suggestions.
* **Today focus card** with the next tiny step (`Open the presentation.`), reminder card, and paper-pinned note.
* **Still on my mind** list with quick actions: *Schedule*, *Remind me*, *Make smaller*, and *Not today*.
* **Three large, friendly actions**: `+ Add something`, `🧠 Brain dump`, and `🛟 I'm stuck`.

### 📅 2. Calendar (Physical Planner Desk)
* A physical planner translated into a digital space—not a cramped grid of numbers.
* Ample space under every date for **Tasks**, **Reminders**, and **Notes**.
* Inline `+ Add item` directly to any date.
* Switch between **Spacious Planner** view and **Month Grid** view.

### 📝 3. Things
* Simple, calm organization into three natural groups:
  * **TODAY** — Things that matter today.
  * **SOON** — Things that matter sometime soon.
  * **LATER** — Things that can wait.
* **Energy requirement tags**: `🌱 Low energy`, `🌿 Medium energy`, `🔥 High energy`.
* **Energy Filter Bar**: Click `🌱 Low energy` to answer *"I have very little energy. What can I do?"*
* **The "Not Today" Philosophy**: Postponing moves tasks quietly into *Later* with zero guilt or red text.

### 🧠 4. Brain Dump
* Large free-form text box: *"You don't have to organize it yet. Just put it here."*
* One-click *"Try example thoughts ✨"* for testing.
* Simulated AI thought extractor separates tasks from noise, offering interactive sorting pills (*Today*, *Soon*, *Later*, *Just remember*) before saving.

### 💭 5. Journal
* *"A place to put the things that aren't tasks. You don't have to make sense here."*
* Private notebook atmosphere with mood badges and personal reflection history.

### 🛟 Unstuck Mode (Immersive Focused Overlay)
* Accessible anytime via `🛟 I'm stuck` or `Make smaller` on any task.
* Unpacks resistance: *Too overwhelming*, *I don't know where to start*, *I have no energy*, *I don't want to*, *I'm distracted*, *Something else is on my mind*.
* Breaks any task down into a ridiculously tiny physical step (`Open the presentation. That's your only job right now.`).
* Includes *"Make it even smaller"* button and gentle validation: *"You moved. 🌱"*.

## 🎵 Sound Design & Ambience

Built with native Web Audio API (zero external assets or network requests):
* **Wooden Marimba Click**: Soft tactile feedback on interactions.
* **Warm Sine Chime**: Gentle bell chime on completion.
* **Procedural Rain & Room Ambience**: Toggleable soothing sound generator right in the header.

## 🚀 Running Locally

This prototype requires zero dependencies and runs in any modern browser.

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/littlellama77/the-spare-room.git
   cd the-spare-room
   ```

2. **Serve with Node.js**:
   ```bash
   node server.js
   ```

3. Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** in your browser.

*(Alternatively, you can open `index.html` directly in your browser).*
